What is worth writing about

  • A result that looks wrong. Include the figures the test reported, what you expected and why, your browser, and whether you were on Wi-Fi or a cable. That context is what makes a report actionable.
  • A bug. Anything that fails to load, a test that never finishes, a layout that breaks on your screen. Mentioning your browser and device helps a great deal.
  • A correction. If something in a guide is wrong or has gone out of date, say so — corrections get made and noted.
  • Advertising and partnerships. Advertising on this site is served through Google AdSense; enquiries about direct placements can go to the same address.
  • Privacy requests. See the privacy policy first — because the site stores nothing on a server, most data requests have a very short answer.

What this address is not for

SpeedMeter is not your internet provider and has no access to your account, your router or your line. If your connection is down or persistently slow, your provider's support team is the only party who can actually investigate it. The guide on slow results is a good thing to read first, so that the conversation with them starts from solid evidence.

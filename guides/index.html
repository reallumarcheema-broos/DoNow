Where to start

If your result came in lower than you expected, begin with why your speed test is slower than your plan — the cause is usually inside your own home, and it is usually Wi-Fi. If the connection is fast but calls and games feel unreliable, ping, jitter and packet loss is the one that will explain it. And if you are deciding whether to pay for an upgrade, how much speed you actually need will probably save you money.

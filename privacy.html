Privacy policy

Short version: this site has no server of its own, no accounts and no analytics. The longer version below covers the two places data does travel — the test itself, and advertising.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Who runs this site

SpeedMeter is an independent website offering a free internet speed test. Questions about this policy can be sent to the address on the contact page.

What we collect

SpeedMeter has no backend server, no user accounts, no sign-up form and no analytics package. We do not collect, store or transmit your name, email address, location or test results. Nothing you do on this site is recorded by us.

What stays in your browser

Two things are saved locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage:

  • Your recent test results — the last eight runs, so you can compare your connection over time. They are shown in the history table on the home page.
  • Your theme preference — whether you chose the light or dark appearance.

This data never leaves your device and is not readable by us. Clearing your browser data, or using the "Clear history" button on the home page, removes it permanently.

The speed test itself

To measure your connection, your browser exchanges data directly with Cloudflare's public speed-test edge endpoints. The payloads are meaningless random bytes generated for the test — never your files or personal data. As with any website you visit, the server at the other end necessarily sees your IP address, approximate location and browser user agent in order to respond at all. That exchange is governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy, not by this one. The connection details shown beneath your result (IP address, provider, nearest edge location) are displayed to you in your browser and are not sent to us or retained.

Cookies and advertising

SpeedMeter itself sets no cookies. However, this site displays advertising served by Google AdSense, and advertising involves third parties that may use cookies and similar technologies.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info, or, in Europe, at Your Online Choices.
  • Advertising partners may collect data such as your IP address, device identifiers and browsing behaviour on this site. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent notice is shown before personalised advertising cookies are used, and you may change or withdraw that consent at any time through the notice's settings link.

Your rights

Because this site stores nothing about you on a server, there is generally no personal data held by us to access, correct, export or delete — the data described above is either on your own device or held by the third parties named above. To exercise rights against those third parties, contact them directly using their policies linked above. Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws you retain the right to access, delete, correct and port your personal data, and to object to its processing or sale; where advertising cookies are concerned, the opt-out links above are the fastest route.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Data transfers and security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is no database to breach. Data handled by Cloudflare and by advertising vendors may be processed in countries other than your own under their respective safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes to how advertising works on the site will be reflected here before they take effect.

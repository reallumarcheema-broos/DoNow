Accuracy of measurements

Results are estimates of the throughput available between your browser and one edge server at one moment in time. They are affected by your Wi-Fi, your device, other traffic on your network, the route to the test server and general congestion. Results should not be treated as a definitive measurement of the capacity of your line, and they are not offered as evidence in any dispute with an internet provider. Always corroborate with several tests and, where it matters, with your provider's own tools.
